# Marketing engineering, showreel (X loop)

**Reference:** `../_references/ajith_showreel.mp4` (@ajith_io, Claude-made motion reel, 15s, 8 bars at 128 BPM).
Replicate its craft and grammar, adapted to one message.

**Message:** Marketing engineering. AI does the work, a human owns the outcome.
**Format:** 1920x1080, 15.000s, 60 fps, music + sparse SFX. Unbranded. Loops on X.
**Copy rules:** plain words, no em or en dashes anywhere on screen.

## Grammar
- 8 bars x 1.875s (128 BPM). Scene change on every downbeat: 0, 1.875, 3.75, 5.625, 7.5, 9.375, 11.25, 13.125.
- One red dot is the through-line (the human). Palette: black #111113, vermilion #E9432F, cream #EEEAE3, cobalt #2E3DF2, yellow #F3C63B.
- Designer frame on top: corner crop marks, "MARKETING ENGINEERING  REEL 2026" top left, beat squares + "BAR n/8" + "128 BPM" top right, frame counter + timecode bottom right. Subtle film grain.
- Type: Montserrat 900 (kinetic), Source Serif 4 (wordmark), Barlow + JetBrains Mono (metadata).
- No glows, no gradients, no bounce eases, no confetti. Motion blur only on fast beats.

## Bars
1. Black. One red dot (the human) spawns a ring of six agent nodes: COPY, ADS, EMAIL, CRM, SEO, DATA. Dot bursts into a vermilion iris.
2. Vermilion. "SHIP [EVERY] WEEK." with brackets snapping; push into the period, black iris.
3. Black. Design-tool bounding box: button, then landing page card, then ad unit, with readouts (ROT, W x H, VARIANT 3/12).
4. Cream. Dot grid = contacts. Outreach waves light them red. "4,812 CONTACTS · WAVE 2". Grid tilts into 3D.
5. Cobalt. 3D point cloud (Three.js) collapses into a pipeline funnel wireframe with stage labels.
6. Yellow. Striped ribbon draws the customer journey: AD, PAGE, CALL, CLOSED.
7. Black. Text bands (ADS · LANDING PAGES · EMAIL · CRM · SEO · VOICE), outline/solid, curl into rotating rings round the red dot.
8. Cream. Red dot lands as the period of "Marketing engineering." + "AI DOES THE WORK · A HUMAN OWNS THE OUTCOME". Last 0.25s returns to black + single red dot (frame 0) for the loop.
